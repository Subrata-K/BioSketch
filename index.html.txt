<h2>About</h2>
<p>I received the Ph.D. degree in Electrical Engineering from Texas Tech University. I am currently a faculty member with the Centre for NEMS and Nanophotonics (CNNP) and with the RF and Photonics research group at IIT-Madras. </p>

<span id="teaching"><h3>Teaching</h3></span>
<p>Introduced EE6506 - Computational Electromagnetics
A graduate level theory + laboratory course (for III semester M.Tech. , M.S. and Ph.D. students) involving hands-on algorithm study, code development and computational assignments to solve basic to advanced problems in classical electromagnetism.
Significant Outcomes</p>
<ol>
<li><p>40 Matlab/Octave programs covering various problems ranging from fields of a Capacitor to Modeling of Photonic Crystals to Demonstration of Surface Plasmons are available to all students and researchers at Our Mathworks Account Link 
<a href="https://in.mathworks.com/matlabcentral/profile/authors/3079258-computational-electromagnetics-at-iit-madras">https://in.mathworks.com/matlabcentral/profile/authors/3079258-computational-electromagnetics-at-iit-madras</a></p>
</li>
<li><p>More than 50 associated videos to show the results of all simulation codes developed in the course are available at <a href="https://www.youtube.com/user/IITMComputationalEM">https://www.youtube.com/user/IITMComputationalEM</a></p>
</li>
</ol>
